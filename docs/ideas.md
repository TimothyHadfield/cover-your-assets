# Ideas: data, odds and theory for Cover Your Assets

Brainstormed 2026-10-19. **Build** = built and live; **Skip** = not built, with the reason.

| # | Idea | Verdict | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Steal odds on every opponent** (chance you win a challenge if both sides play everything, from the cards you haven't seen) | Build | Play: "Steal %" chip on each opponent, red→green |
| 2 | **Top-set risk**: chance someone can challenge / beat your top set, and the dollars at risk | Build | Play: chip under your stack; laptop side panel |
| 3 | **Discard gift risk**: chance the next player can pair the card you're about to throw | Build | Play: message line when one card is picked |
| 4 | **Hint**: the hard computer's best move and why, in dollars | Build | Play: Hint button (turn and fights) |
| 5 | **Coach**: after each of your moves, how many dollars the best move was worth over yours, with a running total | Build | Play toasts + round-end panel; Settings switch |
| 6 | **Real-table tracker**: tap in pairs and steals at a physical table; totals, history, steal chance per top set, cards still unseen | Build | Table tab |
| 7 | **Fight calculator**: your fighters vs their hand size → win chance heatmap | Build | Lab |
| 8 | **Measured computer strength** (thousands of games, seats rotated) | Build | Lab + Settings note |
| 9 | **Is the nest egg worth chasing?** (win rate when your first set is high-value) | Build | Lab |
| 10 | **Which sets get stolen most** (by card type) and **when in a round steals happen** | Build | Lab |
| 11 | **Rounds needed to reach $1M** by player count | Build | Lab |
| 12 | **Seat advantage** (does going first win more?) | Build | Lab |
| 13 | **Deck money map**: where the $1,360,000 sits by card type | Build | Lab |
| 14 | **Hard computer's action mix** (how often it pairs, steals, discards) | Build | Lab |
| 15 | Online rooms where any seat is a person or a computer | Build | Settings › Online (shared Firebase layer) |
| 16 | Card counting overlay listing every card seen so far by every player | Skip | Same numbers as the unseen grid; clutters the phone screen |
| 17 | Bluff/tell model (guess an opponent's hand from what they *didn't* do) | Skip | Needs a reliable model of human play; computer opponents would make it misleading |
| 18 | Exact game-theory solver for fights | Skip | Fights are already solved by counting: the side holding more fighters wins if both play all; the calculator shows this |
| 19 | Team-play strategy stats | Skip | Teams are a house rule with no published source; stats would describe our own rule only |
| 20 | Newest printing's asset names (Comic Books, Piano, ...) as a third deck | Skip | Card values not found in any source; would be guesswork (see rules-check.md) |
